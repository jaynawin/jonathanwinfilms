About Me

My Journey

Hello! My name is Jonathan Nguyen, and I am a filmmaker, researcher, and advocate passionate about storytelling, social justice, and community engagement. As a first-generation low-income student, I have navigated complex systems firsthand, fueling my commitment to equity and meaningful change. My academic background in Cinema & Media Studies and Biology allows me to approach issues with both analytical precision and creative expression, whether through scientific research, filmmaking, or public advocacy.

My work spans multiple disciplines—from studying racial disparities in healthcare at Georgetown University to producing documentary and visual projects that highlight social issues. I have filmed fundraising videos for nonprofit organizations, collaborated on visual journalism pieces, and explored themes of memory, identity, and change through experimental storytelling. Beyond my creative and academic pursuits, I actively engage in community-driven initiatives, leading efforts to combat food insecurity, mentoring local youth, and organizing events on social justice issues.

I believe in the power of art, research, and advocacy to bridge gaps, amplify voices, and foster meaningful dialogue. Whether behind a camera, in a lab, or working directly with communities, I am dedicated to using my skills to drive impact and tell stories that matter.

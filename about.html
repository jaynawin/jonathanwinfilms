About Me

Hi, I'm Jonathan.

I'm a filmmaker, researcher, an aspiring physician, and a recent graduate from Carleton College, where I studied Biology and Cinema & Media Studies.

My work is connected by a common focus: understanding people's stories. I'm interested in how personal experiences intersect with larger social, cultural, and healthcare systems, and how storytelling can help bridge the gap between data and lived experience.

Through documentary filmmaking, health equity research, and community engagement, I've worked to better understand how people make meaning of their experiences and how those experiences are represented. Whether I'm conducting research or making a film, my goal is the same: to listen carefully, learn from others, and share stories with thoughtfulness and care.
